<package_scope>
    A building block of Core and Local, not referenced directly by apps: published on nuget.org as their dependency, one version with every Axiom.Mesh package. Namespace Axiom.Mesh.Protocol (folders for orientation only).
    Amp: Alpn "axiom-mesh/1", Version 1, the state names Connected, Established, Closed, and Definition (built once, symmetric): u32 length-prefixed frames of 2 to 4 198 400 bytes, a hand-written frame codec (u16 id; 0 and above 255 are violations, 17 to 255 decode as Extension and are consumed silently once established; a malformed Bye reads as Bye(Unspecified), a close), Connected accepts Hello and Bye and sends Hello, Ping, Bye; Established accepts and sends every message but Hello, answers Ping with Pong and ServiceOpen with ServiceRefused(Unknown), consumes Pong and Extension, and treats ServiceAccepted/ServiceRefused as violations (phase 1 never opens a service); a Bye sent or received enters the final Closed state; FirstMessageTimeout 10 s and IdleTimeout 5 min (both reply Bye(Idle)), Heartbeat Ping after 60 s without sending (counter nonce), every other violation replies Bye(Violation), closing sends Bye(Shutdown), every write flushed.
    Messages: MeshMessage (closed; Id, Write) and Hello, Packages(PackageState), PackageChanged, Have, Want, ObjectMessage, NotFound, Cancel, RosterQuery, RosterNotFound, ServiceOpen, ServiceAccepted, ServiceRefused, Ping, Pong, Bye, plus Extension; MessageIds, ByeReason, Capabilities (reserved bits dropped on read, refused on write), ServiceRefusal. Every message implements IWireMessage, checks every bound of SPEC.md §7 through Wire and Limits before reading content, copies lists into owned arrays, and validates its arguments when constructed.
    ObjectMessage: From(StorableObject) only, no copy; a received one has its hash verified on decode (mismatch is a violation), is a zero-copy view of the frame stamped to the read, exposes Hash, Bytes and Object (a HashedObject for Objects.TryParse), and throws StaleMessageException after the next read (IsCurrent says so).
    Rules: HelloRule.Check(Hello, PeerIdentityFacts(TlsKey, HeldRosterSeq), ITrustSnapshotView) → HelloOutcome.Ok(TrustedDevice, RosterFollowUp None|SendHeldRoster|QueryRoster) or HelloOutcome.Refused(Bye(Unauthorized)). ListingRule (immutable; OnPackages, OnPackageChanged, Deadline, IsOverdue → ListingStep{Verdict Accepted|Completed|Violation, Next, Detail, Bye}). WantBudget (64 outstanding; TryWant, Settle, Remaining). ObjectQueueRule (8 MiB of object bytes; TryQueue, Dequeue, DefersWants).
    Pairing: PairProtocol (Alpn "axiom-mesh-pair/1", ids 1 to 3, states Connected, Exchanged, PeerConfirmed, Closed, Definition with Hello 10 s and idle 10 min), PairHello, PairConfirm (Plain or Enrolling(VerifiedRoster)), PairCancel(PairCancelReason), PairingCode (TryParse, Text, Mode, EnteringMode, Addresses, DeviceKey) with PairingAddress, PairingTarget (OfCode, OfDevice(DeviceId, addresses), Device, Addresses, Code?, IsByCode, Admits(PublicKey) and Admits(point, out key): the entering side's pin is the device id, H of the key the handshake must present, PAIR-SPEC 2.4), Sas.Derive(userKeyA?, tlsKeyA, userKeyB?, tlsKeyB) → "XXXX-XXXX", PairingRules (TryAcceptHello → PairPeer, TryAcceptEnrolment → VerifiedRoster, TryEnrol → RosterDraft, Counterpart), PairingMachine (immutable; TryStart, OnPeerHello, OnPeerRefused, OnConfirm, OnCancel, OnPeerConfirm, OnPeerCancel, OnDisconnected, OnTick → PairingStep{Result Ok|Invalid|NotFound, Next, Action None|SendConfirm|SendEnrolment|SendCancel, Cancel}), PairingMode and PairingState with the values of LOCAL-SPEC.md §14.10.
</package_scope>

<architecture>
    Honest: definitions, codecs and pure rules over Model's types; depends on Model and ProtoStream only. Unknown message ids go through .Framing(...).FrameCodec(...), not .Messages(...), so ids 17 to 255 are ignored as SPEC.md 1.4 says (plan decision D5); the pairing protocol has no extension range and uses .Messages(...) with a u16 discriminator. Every rule takes facts and timestamps and returns a value; nothing reads a clock, a socket or a random source. The heartbeat nonce is a counter captured by the definition.
</architecture>

<best_practices>
    Write the own Hello before the first read, then check the peer's with HelloRule.Check before anything else; send the Refused outcome's Bye. Feed ListingRule every Packages and PackageChanged with the session's time and arm a timer for Deadline; count received Wants with WantBudget.TryWant and settle them when answered or cancelled; ask ObjectQueueRule.TryQueue before queueing an answer and defer the want when it says no. Parse or copy a received ObjectMessage before the next read. In pairing, refuse a PairHello with PairingRules.TryAcceptHello's PairCancel, derive the SAS from the keys the TLS handshake proved, check TryEnrol when the device's PairHello arrives (AddDevice), and act on PairingStep.Action. Round-trip every message at every byte split with CodecHarness; refuse every bound one over and accept it at the bound.
</best_practices>

<pitfalls>
    A violation closes with Bye; faulty content does not (SPEC.md 12): a malformed object inside a valid Object message is the caller's ObjectFault, not a violation. The definition changes state on receipt: Established means the peer's Hello arrived, not that it passed HelloRule. The 30-second frame completion of SPEC.md 5.1 is not enforced by the definition (ProtoStream bounds a frame by the 5-minute idle timeout); the session handler must add it. Inbound Ping before the peer's Hello is a violation (SPEC.md 6.1). Protocol is a namespace inside Axiom.Mesh: write global::Axiom.ProtoStream.Protocol.Describe when building a definition there.
</pitfalls>
