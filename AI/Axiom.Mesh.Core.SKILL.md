<architecture>
    One LWSE server per node with three ports (DESIGN.md 10.2): the mesh port (bound, or a number in memory) Through(MeshTls server).Speak(AMP/1); Ports.Outbound, adopt-only, Through(MeshTls client).Speak(AMP/1) for every dial; Ports.Local, adopt-only, Speak(axiom-mesh-local/1). ServerOptions.Admission.PerClient = 4. NodeModule registers the protocols, the transport, the observer and four LoopServices (dialler, discovery, trust, evictor) that wait until the ports are bound. MeshTls: TLS 1.3, client certificate required, AllowTlsResume false, no renegotiation, no chain or revocation check, a 10 s handshake deadline on the node clock; PeerCheck accepts in the callback only a key TrustSnapshot.TryResolveDevice resolves (or, while a pairing waits, a pairing candidate), and Decide after the handshake admits a roster key under axiom-mesh/1 and a pairing key under axiom-mesh-pair/1 only. PeerSessionHandler writes its Hello first, checks the peer's with HelloRule.Check against one snapshot, applies the duplicate rule through PeerTable.Establish (the loser hears Bye(Duplicate)), sends the Roster follow-up, then reads until the session ends, is asked to close (Duplicate, Unauthorized) or the node drains (Bye(Shutdown)). PeerTable (one lock, no I/O inside, an immutable view, facts recorded after the lock) is fed by DiscoveryPump (announcements; after a trust change it closes untrusted sessions and offers CompositeDiscovery.Cache.Snapshot()), by PeerDialler (TryReserve, IDialer.DialAsync, AdoptAsync, DialEnded) and by sessions; a backoff schedules a retry timer that re-offers the device at its last address. One writer, immutable snapshots, per-session actors (plan decision D8), as in M5.
</architecture>

<best_practices>
    Start a node only through MeshNode.StartAsync with every seam in MeshNodeEnvironment; trust enters only by AdoptIdentityAsync and AcceptUserAsync (and later the pairing shell). Read trust through the writer's current snapshot, never a ChangeSet's (it may be stale when read). Decide with the pure rules (DialPolicy, Backoff, ViolationWindow, DuplicateSessions, HelloRule) and keep the table's lock free of I/O and waiting. Record a NodeFact for every decision about a connection or a peer, after the state it reports is published. Store only through AdmitCommand with a StorableObject; for objects from peers go through Intake. Every limit comes from Model's Limits.
</best_practices>

<pitfalls>
    On .NET 8 (Windows) the certificate callback cannot read the negotiated ALPN; PeerCheck.Decide after the handshake is the authority (STRUCTURAL-DEBT 21). A pairing-ALPN connection reaches the AMP/1 handler, which aborts it (debt 22). SslStream on Windows reports LocalCertificate and the client side's IsMutuallyAuthenticated unreliably with ClientCertificateContext; do not decide on them. An established session drops what it receives until M7 (debt 24); the 30-second frame bound is not enforced yet (debt 20). A parsed object refers to the memory it was parsed from: copy a frame before parsing. LDB 0.1.0 has one [Key] per entity: multi-part keys are concatenated (RowKeys).
</pitfalls>
